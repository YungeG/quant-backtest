# Binance USDⓈ-M Historical Funding Source Semantics Primary Sources

## Scope

This note records the first-party facts used to freeze G10E. G10E is a pure offline adapter from caller-supplied immutable Binance USDⓈ-M funding-history evidence into the already-frozen G09C funding-publication and G09D funding-accounting seams.

G10E does not download data, call REST or WebSocket APIs, parse provider files, infer account positions, calculate Binance's premium index, reconstruct a missing rate, append a Journal entry, prove archive completeness, authorize live trading, or perform parity against a real Binance account. G10G owns profile composition; G12 owns acquisition, checksums, retention, revision history, and complete slot-coverage proof.

## Funding Rate History is the accepted v1 authority

Binance documents `GET /fapi/v1/fundingRate` as the USDⓈ-M funding-rate-history endpoint. A response row includes:

- `symbol`;
- `fundingRate`;
- `fundingTime` in milliseconds;
- `markPrice`, described as the mark price associated with that particular funding-fee charge;
- in the newer documented response, `rateType`, where `Regular` is the normal funding rate and `Special` is an additional funding rate generated by stock dividends.

The endpoint returns rows in ascending order. Query `startTime` and `endTime` are inclusive. Omitting both returns only recent rows, so a current endpoint response is not historical completeness evidence and must never backfill a frozen run.

G10E v1 accepts only one immutable `Regular` row for an exact stable Instrument and `fundingTime`. It does not sum, net, or otherwise transform `Special` rows. A `Special` row represents an additional economic event that cannot share G09C's `(instrument, target_funding_time)` slot identity without changing the frozen generic contract; it therefore fails closed in v1.

- USDⓈ-M Funding Rate History: <https://developers.binance.com/legacy-docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-History>
- Chinese USDⓈ-M Funding Rate History: <https://developers.binance.com/docs/zh-CN/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-History>

## Funding slot and settlement time

`fundingTime` is the provider's funding time for the row. G10E derives the existing G09C `FundingSlotId` only from the stable G10A `InstrumentId` and that exact UTC millisecond. It does not assume a fixed eight-hour cadence, infer a missing slot from neighboring rows, or derive slot identity from symbol text, rate, mark, interval, account, source revision, or archive path.

Binance's Funding Info endpoint exposes `fundingIntervalHours` only for symbols whose funding cap, floor, or interval was specially adjusted; unadjusted symbols are omitted. The response is current market data and does not provide historical effective intervals. It is therefore not a v1 historical slot authority. Actual `fundingTime` rows remain authoritative, which also preserves historical one-hour, four-hour, eight-hour, or other provider schedules without inference.

Source:

- USDⓈ-M Funding Info: <https://developers.binance.com/legacy-docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Get-Funding-Rate-Info>

## Final historical rate versus predicted/current rate

The Mark Price Stream publishes distinct fields:

- `r`: funding rate;
- `T`: next funding time;
- `p`: mark price;
- `ap`: moving-average mark;
- `i`: index price;
- `P`: estimated settlement price.

The current Mark Price REST response similarly exposes `lastFundingRate` and `nextFundingTime`. These current or streaming fields are not immutable historical charge rows. G10E does not use them as final historical publication authority and does not use `r`/`T`, `lastFundingRate`, cap/floor, interest rate, or premium-index inputs to recompute a historical applied rate.

The accepted `fundingRate` string from the exact Funding Rate History row maps directly to G09C/G09D basis `funding_fraction_of_notional`. No percentage scaling, interval annualization, cap application, sign inversion, or formula replay is performed by the adapter.

Sources:

- USDⓈ-M Mark Price Stream: <https://developers.binance.com/legacy-docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/websocket-market-streams/Mark-Price-Stream>
- USDⓈ-M Mark Price REST response: <https://developers.binance.com/legacy-docs/derivatives/usds-margined-futures/market-data/rest-api/Mark-Price>

## Associated funding mark

The Funding Rate History documentation explicitly calls `markPrice` the mark price associated with a particular funding-fee charge. G10E maps only that field, from the same accepted row, to `PricePurpose.FUNDING` at the exact `fundingTime`.

It does not substitute:

- a nearby G10D mark-price-kline close;
- a live or archived ordinary mark-price update;
- index price;
- moving-average mark;
- estimated settlement price;
- contract trade price;
- last price.

The resulting generic mark has zero economic age at the slot and uses a frozen no-forward-fill, zero-max-age `StaleMarkPolicy`. The mark must be positive and exactly representable at the supplied G09A contract price scale. G10E does not round or rescale an inexact provider mark because G09D's exact cash-flow contract consumes the mark before its single final Money quantization boundary.

## Deterministic publication and application ordering

Binance supplies millisecond `fundingTime`, not the repository's intra-instant phase ordering. G10E therefore freezes a simulation convention rather than claiming a provider matching-engine phase:

- target/effective UTC is the exact `fundingTime`;
- G09C historical position eligibility remains at its frozen `TimelinePhase(100, "funding_eligibility")`;
- the final Funding Rate History row becomes publication/settlement evidence at `TimelinePhase(110, "funding_settlement")`, `SourceSequence(0)` at the same UTC;
- G09D settlement `applied_at` is that same deterministic instant.

This ordering uses the G09C cutoff state strictly before eligibility and applies funding only after that boundary. Archive capture or revision visibility remains separate caller-supplied `SimulationInstant` evidence. A row is unavailable to a G10E query until its immutable source revision is captured, but later archive acquisition does not change the historical economic `fundingTime`.

G10E v1 accepts one root immutable source revision per slot. A superseding row or two different visible rows for the same regular slot fail closed. This avoids pretending that a later archival correction was published at the original funding instant or silently rewriting a previously frozen result. G12 must preserve the complete source-revision history before this policy can be widened.

## Account settlement streams are not source-rate authority

Binance user-data documentation states that an `ACCOUNT_UPDATE` event may be emitted for reason `FUNDING_FEE`, and distinguishes event time `E` from transaction time `T`. Such account-specific events are useful for later parity and account-history verification, but they are not required to calculate a synthetic backtest account's funding from public market evidence.

G10E v1 therefore does not consume authenticated account streams or Income History and does not use actual account balance changes to select the public rate or mark. It produces the provider evidence required by G09C/G09D; G09D computes the synthetic account cash flow from the historical eligible position, multiplier, associated mark, and applied rate.

## Exact decimal and identity rules

G10E preserves raw provider decimal strings in source identity. The rate grammar permits canonical zero, positive, or negative ordinary decimal strings; the mark grammar permits only a positive ordinary decimal string. Neither permits whitespace, leading plus, exponent notation, NaN/Infinity, Unicode alternatives, or more than the repository's supported fractional precision.

Mapping uses string/integer arithmetic only. Raw trailing zeros remain source evidence, while generic `Rate` and `Price` use exact integer units and `Scale`. Float, ambient `Decimal` context, `pricePrecision`, inferred tick precision, and pre-quantization are forbidden.

Every normalized result retains:

- stable G10A Instrument identity and full supplied G09A contract;
- exact provider `fundingTime`;
- raw `fundingRate`, `markPrice`, and `rateType`;
- source event ID and canonical event hash;
- source key, content hash, archive key/path, revision ID, and capture provenance;
- derived G09C Slot ID;
- G09C publication candidate;
- purpose-specific Funding mark and frozen stale policy;
- account-specific G09D settlement evidence bound to the caller-supplied Application Key;
- a G10E model digest and `decision_grade_eligible=false`.

## Coverage, current APIs, and archive limitations

Binance Data Collection exposes a first-party USD-M funding-rate archive directory. Its existence is source-family evidence, not proof that every expected slot, symbol, revision, or checksum required by a run is present.

G10E validates a caller-declared finite half-open coverage band and exact target row. It does not infer missing rows from a standard schedule. It does not use the latest REST response, Funding Info, current Mark Price, neighboring funding rows, or a nearby mark kline to fill a gap.

G12 must retain and prove the exact files, checksums, initial state, all revisions, and gap classification. Until that proof exists, G10E output remains development-grade and `decision_grade_eligible=false`.

Source:

- Binance Data Collection funding-rate directory: <https://data.binance.vision/?prefix=data/futures/um/monthly/fundingRate/>

## Frozen G10E boundary

The smallest sufficient implementation seam is one pure offline module under `crypto_quant_trading.profiles.binance_usdm` that:

- consumes a G10A resolution, a full caller-supplied G09A linear-perpetual contract, an account/slot G09D Application Key, one finite historical funding book, target funding UTC, and capture instant;
- accepts exactly one visible immutable `Regular` Funding Rate History row for the target slot;
- derives the existing G09C Slot ID from stable Instrument plus `fundingTime`;
- maps the exact row rate to one G09C final-rate publication;
- maps the same row's associated mark to a zero-age `PricePurpose.FUNDING` resolution with no fallback;
- maps the row to G09D settlement evidence at the frozen post-eligibility settlement phase;
- preserves source, revision, timing, decimal, account, contract, and model identity;
- returns structured failure for missing, unavailable, special, revised, duplicate, malformed, inexact, cross-context, or forged evidence.

The adapter does not alter generic Funding eligibility, Funding accounting, MarkResolver, Journal, Ledger, Engine, Runner, or Timeline behavior.

## Known limitations retained by G10E

- `Special` funding rows are unsupported because the frozen Slot/Application identity cannot represent an additional same-time funding event without a generic contract change.
- G10E does not reconstruct Binance's funding-rate formula or historical funding-interval configuration.
- A later source supersession is rejected rather than retroactively published at the original slot.
- Account `FUNDING_FEE` events and Income History are parity/account evidence, not v1 public source authority.
- Archive presence is not gap-free completeness; G12 remains required.
- All output remains development-grade and `decision_grade_eligible=false` until G12 proves immutable source retention and complete slot coverage.
- Direct Binance documentation fetches in the development environment resolve through a fake-IP range and are blocked by SSRF protection; the cited first-party pages were preserved through searchable official content.
