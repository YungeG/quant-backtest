# G12H XSHE domestic ordinary-A-share latest-document sufficiency v1

## Scope and decision rule

**Target:** Asia/Shanghai `[2026-07-06, 2026-07-31)`, XSHE, `InstrumentType.EQUITY`, CNY quote and settlement, `AUCTION`, every board admitted by the bound profile, `DOMESTIC`, `ORDINARY_A_SHARE`, `trade_notional`.

This is requirements-first research. Per the effective-until-authoritatively-superseded plan and the ADR-0004/0005 semantics it incorporates, a rate page is not a closure: each lineage needs a competent official act/representation, authoritative effective and supersession facts, all economic fields, a dated official endpoint at or after `2026-07-31`, complete correction/successor channel coverage through a chosen record cutoff, and immutable official bytes/receipts/hashes. A current undated page, `全文有效`, or an empty search result does not prove continuity. Documentary correction (a later representation of the *same* act) is evaluated separately from economic succession (a later complete applicable act/state).

**Result: FAIL CLOSED.** No located document is demonstrated to be a competent official endpoint recorded at or after target end, and no lineage has a complete correction/successor index or captured official bytes. Therefore none is sufficient for F1 closure, even where the candidate economics are well supported.

Status: **SATISFIED** = directly stated by a cited first-party source for this candidate state; **MISSING** = absent or cannot meet the closure requirement; **AMBIGUOUS** = the source suggests the fact but does not express the exact requested tuple.

## Latest-document evaluation and minimal backward trace

### 1. `exchange_handling` — insufficient

**Latest located:** the SZSE table is labelled “深交所收费及代收税费标准（2026年1月）.” Its A-share row says “按成交额双边收取0.0341‰”; note 6 says it changed from 0.0487‰ to 0.0341‰ on 2023-08-28. [SZSE fee table](https://www.szse.cn/marketServices/deal/payFees/index.html). This directly supports the candidate rate, notional basis and bilateral side. It does **not** prove validity through 2026-07-31.

**Necessary backward document only:** capture the exact [2023 SZSE reduction notice](https://www.szse.cn/disclosure/notice/general/t20230818_602805.html), because it is the immediately referenced rate-change act and fills the table’s missing act identity/effective-transition evidence. No broader historical fee collection is justified.

**Still required:** a fee table/validity record dated at or after 2026-07-31; complete SZSE fee-notice/table-revision/correction/repeal index to the chosen `official_record_as_of`; candidate dispositions; raw and rendered table/notice bytes, headers, redirect receipts, and hashes. The 2026 Trading Rules are not a fee successor: Arts. 9.2–9.3 merely refer to fees “according to relevant provisions.” [2026 Trading Rules](https://docs.static.szse.cn/www/lawrules/rule/trade/current/W020260424690713155663.pdf).

### 2. `securities_regulatory` — insufficient

**Latest applicable documents:** NDRC/MOF 发改价格规〔2018〕917 states that securities business regulatory fees collected from Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges are 0.02‰ of stock transaction value and takes effect 2018-01-01, while abolishing 发改价格〔2016〕14号. [917](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xxgk/zcfb/ghxwj/201806/t20180627_960950.html?code=). The latest located XSHE collection table independently lists A-share regulatory levy as “按成交额双边收取0.02‰” and “代中国证监会收取.” [SZSE table](https://www.szse.cn/marketServices/deal/payFees/index.html). This split is material: 917 controls the rate but states an exchange payor, whereas the SZSE representation supplies investor bilateral collection.

**Necessary backward trace:** none beyond 917. It expressly disposes of 2016 No.14, so acquiring 2016 No.14 would not fill a target field. The later [NDRC/MOF 2021 No.1947](https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/xxgk/zcfb/tz/202201/t20220107_1311590_ext.html) is a necessary *candidate-disposition* item, not a historic rate source: it says Beijing uses the Shanghai/Shenzhen standard and that standards may later be adjusted; it does not create a complete XSHE successor state.

**Still required:** post-target dated endpoint and complete successor/correction indexes for both (a) the NDRC/MOF controlling-rate stream and (b) the SZSE collection-table stream. Their official bytes and hashes must be separately retained; equal economics cannot merge their documentary chains.

### 4. `hkscc_transfer` — insufficient, with a supported domestic non-applicability candidate

**Latest located:** HKSCC’s current [Operational Procedures index](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Services/Rules-and-Forms-and-Fees/Rules/HKSCC/Operational-Procedures?sc_lang=en) links [Section 21](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Rules-and-Forms-and-Fees/Rules/HKSCC/Operational-Procedures/SEC21.pdf). §21.1A says the 0.002% transfer fee is payable by each China Connect Clearing Participant for each China Connect Securities Trade cleared and settled through HKSCC. The official Northbound fee table says the same and describes it as in addition to Exchange Rules transfer fees. [HKEX Northbound table](https://www.hkex.com.hk/Services/Rules-and-Forms-and-Fees/Fees/Securities-(Stock-Connect)/Clearing-and-Settlement?sc_lang=en).

**Necessary linked rule (not broad history):** the current official [SEHK Chapter 1 definition](https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Rules-and-Forms-and-Fees/Rules/SEHK/Securities/Rules/chap_1_eng.pdf?la=en): a China Connect Securities Trade is executed on a China Connect Market through an SEHK Subsidiary under a Trading Link. This is exactly the missing predicate required to label the requested DOMESTIC XSHE auction `applies=false`. It is a route exclusion, **not** a zero fee and not a successor to ChinaClear.

**Still required:** the dated/versioned post-target representation of Section 21 and definition, plus the complete HKSCC OP/General Rules amendment and circular channel. A July-2026 HKSCC circular found by search is a China-Connect eligibility notice, not a complete fee-rule endpoint or index. Do not equate it with a documentary correction or economic successor.

### 5. `stamp_duty` — insufficient

**Latest applicable documents:** the [Stamp Tax Law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5193058/content.html) defines securities transaction, imposes the tax only on the transferor (Art. 3), uses transaction amount (Art. 5(4)), provides 1‰ in its schedule, begins 2022-07-01, and repeals the prior regulation (Art. 20). [MOF/STA Announcement 2023 No.39](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5211343/content.html) states that securities-transaction stamp duty is half-collected from 2023-08-28. Together they support the candidate 0.5‰ seller-only trade-notional result. The announcement’s current “全文有效” page state is not a record date or a complete successor index.

**Necessary backward act only:** the Law. Announcement 39 alone cannot establish the base statutory rate, seller side or basis. No former stamp-duty regulation should be collected because the Law directly says it repealed it and it would not fill a target-state field.

**Still required:** a competent official legislative/policy endpoint recorded at/after 2026-07-31; complete NPC/MOF/STA amendment, validity, repeal and correction channel(s); documentary comparison of STA and government reproductions before calling either a correction; and immutable official page/attachment/index bytes, receipts and hashes.

1. a competent issuer’s dated endpoint/table or complete official register with `official_record_as_of >= 2026-07-31T00:00:00+08:00`;
