Constructors reject malformed types and non-canonical text only. They do not reject supported-scope, status, tax, withholding, availability, trigger, entitlement, delivered-value, or Lot semantic mismatches; those are structured translator outcomes.

### Cash success contract

`translate_corporate_action_cash_payment()` returns `CnAShareCashPaymentOutcome` containing its complete request and exactly one Journal entry or failure. Success requires a positive payable cash leg, exact entitlement/evidence identities, supported confirmed status, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, CNY Scale-2 zero withholding, `net_cash == gross_cash`, all three immediate-availability flags true, the exact frozen Payment boundary, `available_at == trigger_at`, and `recorded_at >= trigger_at` under full `SimulationInstant` total order.

The Journal entry is exactly:

- `entry_type=AccountingEntryType.CORPORATE_ACTION_CASH_PAID`;
- effective time `evidence.trigger_at.instant`;
- one positive `BalanceChange` for `request.cash_key` and `evidence.net_cash`;
- `position_lot_changes=()`, `realized_pnl=()`, `fees=()`, `financing=()`;
- no Settlement, availability mutation, or partial output.

### Share success contract

`translate_corporate_action_share_delivery()` succeeds only for a positive XSHE bonus/capitalization payable leg, confirmed status, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, CNY Scale-2 zero withholding, `sellable=True`, the exact frozen Listing boundary, `available_at == trigger_at`, `recorded_at >= trigger_at` under full `SimulationInstant` total order, whole Scale-0 quantities whose separate values exact-match G08F entitlement, and exactly one eligible current Lot.

The eligible Lot must exact-match the entitlement account/Venue/Instrument position identity, have a positive current quantity and non-null positive `unit_cost`, and carry strictly positive authoritative CNY Scale-2 `total_cost_basis`. Its current quantity is not required to equal G08F `registered_quantity`: post-Record sales may change current Lot state without changing entitlement. Zero or multiple candidate Lots fail closed; G08G does not allocate across Lots.

Success creates one `AccountingJournalEntry`:

- `entry_type=AccountingEntryType.CORPORATE_ACTION_POSITION_ADJUSTED`;
- effective time `evidence.trigger_at.instant`;
- one position `BalanceChange` equal to bonus plus capitalization delivered shares;
- exactly one `PositionLotChange(before=old_lot, after=adjusted_lot)`;
- no realized PnL, fees, financing, Settlement, or availability mutation.

The replacement preserves Lot ID, source ID, position key, opened time, allocated fees, and authoritative exact `total_cost_basis`; it increases current quantity by delivered shares and derives only non-authoritative `unit_cost` from conserved total basis divided by new quantity using the caller-supplied `QuantizationPolicy`. V1 requires the policy target scale to equal the prior Lot unit-cost scale and the quantized result to remain positive; otherwise `UNIT_COST_QUANTIZATION_MISMATCH` wins. The translator does not return `open_lots`; `AccountingJournal` append and `GenericLedger` replay are authoritative.

### Exact failure contract and precedence

`CnAShareCorporateActionTranslationFailureCode` declaration and guard order are exact:

1. `CONTEXT_MISMATCH = "context_mismatch"`
2. `ENTITLEMENT_EVIDENCE_MISMATCH = "entitlement_evidence_mismatch"`
3. `UNSUPPORTED_ACTION_SCOPE = "unsupported_action_scope"`
4. `UNSUPPORTED_DELIVERY_STATUS = "unsupported_delivery_status"`
5. `UNSUPPORTED_TAX_DISPOSITION = "unsupported_tax_disposition"`
6. `NONZERO_WITHHOLDING = "nonzero_withholding"`
7. `UNSUPPORTED_AVAILABILITY = "unsupported_availability"`
8. `TRIGGER_MISMATCH = "trigger_mismatch"`
9. `EVIDENCE_NOT_AVAILABLE = "evidence_not_available"`
10. `UNSUPPORTED_FRACTIONAL_SHARE = "unsupported_fractional_share"`
11. `DELIVERED_VALUE_MISMATCH = "delivered_value_mismatch"`
12. `EARLY_INVOCATION = "early_invocation"`
13. `ELIGIBLE_LOT_CARDINALITY_MISMATCH = "eligible_lot_cardinality_mismatch"`
14. `LOT_STATE_MISMATCH = "lot_state_mismatch"`
15. `EXACT_COST_BASIS_MISMATCH = "exact_cost_basis_mismatch"`
16. `UNIT_COST_QUANTIZATION_MISMATCH = "unit_cost_quantization_mismatch"`

`UNSUPPORTED_ACTION_SCOPE` covers absent or zero payable legs, XSHG share delivery, and non-XSHE share actions. `EVIDENCE_NOT_AVAILABLE` exact-covers `available_at != trigger_at`. `DELIVERED_VALUE_MISMATCH` exact-covers cash `gross_cash`/`net_cash` versus G08F entitlement after the earlier withholding guard, and share bonus/capitalization value mismatch after the earlier fractional guard. Evidence-intrinsic defects therefore remain stable across an early versus on-time invocation. `ELIGIBLE_LOT_CARDINALITY_MISMATCH` checks absolute `len(open_lots) != 1`; it never filters candidates before cardinality, and identity/state defects of the single Lot are `LOT_STATE_MISMATCH`. All semantic failures return only the first applicable code and no Journal entry or Lot prefix. Duplicate Journal IDs, conflicting replay, and stale/mismatched replay before-state are not translation failures; existing `AccountingJournal` and `GenericLedger` reject them during append/replay.

Each concrete outcome embeds its request and enforces strict XOR. Outcome construction rejects both-present and both-absent states, verifies a failure is the first applicable failure, and verifies a success Journal can be reconstructed exactly from the request.
