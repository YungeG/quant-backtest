# G12A SourceSnapshot Contract

## Outcome

A deterministic, content-addressed, in-memory snapshot of caller-supplied raw source members with separately hashed acquisition provenance and no partial success.

## Readiness work

Research authority: `docs/research/g12a-source-snapshot-contract.md`.

The detailed Gate contract is authoritative in the G12A Acceptance Card in `docs/implementation/acceptance-matrix.md`; this plan does not duplicate status.

Freeze:

- the exact G00-compatible deterministic gzip/USTAR archive recipe;
- portable logical member keys and normalized modes;
- content identity versus acquisition provenance identity;
- structured freeze/verify failure precedence;
- in-memory verified member access for G12B;
- offline stdlib-only core boundaries.

## Dependencies

G00 is PASSED. G12A uses the proven WP-00C archive identity as a contract precedent but does not import migration tooling or its SourceMap/PyYAML/persistence behavior.

Target one separate module: `crypto_quant_bundle_builder.source_snapshots`.

## Exclusions

G12A does not acquire from network/filesystem/provider APIs, normalize records, publish to a repository, prove retention/retrievability/completeness, or authorize decision-grade/runtime use. G12D owns durable atomic publication; G12L owns provider acquisition.
