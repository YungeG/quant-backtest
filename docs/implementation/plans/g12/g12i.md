# G12I Price, Availability, and Revision Coverage

## Outcome

Eventually produce exact price-purpose, market-availability, and revision-provenance reports from declared immutable closure evidence, without synthetic data or provider inference.

## Current status

`DRAFT / BLOCKED`. G12C/G12G provide structural and mechanical evidence only. No Builder-consumable authority currently declares required price purposes, projects G03 stale policy, classifies every gap, or closes revision terminal sets.

Research authority: `docs/research/g12i-price-availability-revision-coverage.md`.

## Blockers to resolve first

Freeze three immutable declaration contracts:

1. required price-purpose/stream/Instrument/time scopes plus a canonical stale-policy projection with test-only parity to Kernel `StaleMarkPolicy`;
2. explicit availability spans/reasons/source identity/empty coverage linked to G12G mechanics;
3. exact revision lineage and terminal-set closure, including explicit empty scope and causal limit.

Builder must consume these projections without importing Trading Kernel. Empty Bars or valid local revision chains cannot establish real-world completeness.

## Provisional implementation seam

After prerequisites pass, one pure Builder analyzer may return all three reports atomically or one structured failure. Final public names, canonical bodies, failure precedence, and RED commands remain unfrozen until the declarations are approved.

## Readiness checks

Before changing `readiness` to `READY`, evidence must prove:

- immutable profile/build ownership of required purposes;
- exact stale-policy projection parity;
- explicit gap reason vocabulary and complete non-overlapping declaration;
- terminal revision closure and explicit empty semantics;
- no cross-purpose fallback or execution/liquidation forward fill;
- deterministic failures, atomicity, repeat hashes, and Builder-only imports.

## Non-goals

No provider truth, calendar derivation, gap invention, Bar generation, interpolation, revision repair, Runtime/Kernel production import, Engine branch, decision-grade claim, live use, or deployment authorization.
