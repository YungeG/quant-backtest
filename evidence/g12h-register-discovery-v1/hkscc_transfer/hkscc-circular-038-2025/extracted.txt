Subject:         Amendments to the HKSCC Operational Procedures (“Operational Procedures”)
                 of Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited (“HKSCC”)

Enquiry:         CCASS Hotline1: 2979 7111 / E-mail: clearingpsd@hkex.com.hk



Participants of HKSCC are requested to note that the Securities and Futures Commission has
approved amendments to the Operational Procedures of HKSCC for the purpose of changing
the rate of interest allowed or charged to Participants on marks, margin or collateral.

The amendments, as set out in the Appendix, will come into effect on Thursday, 2 October 2025.

The marked-up version of the amendments to the Operational Procedures can be downloaded
from the "Rule Update - HKSCC Operational Procedures” section of the HKEX website.




Beatrice Lung
Head of Legal
