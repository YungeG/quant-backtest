Subject:         Amendments to the HKSCC Operational Procedures (“Operational Procedures”)
                 of Hong Kong Securities Clearing Company Limited (“HKSCC”)

Enquiry:         CCASS Hotline1 : 2979 7111 / E-mail: clearingps@hkex.com.hk



Participants of HKSCC are requested to note that the Securities and Futures Commission has
approved amendments to the Operational Procedures of HKSCC for the purposes of revising
the existing stock settlement fee for exchange trades and crossed exchange trades payable to
HKSCC and introducing a separate rate for eligible exchange trades and crossed exchange
trades in designated exchange traded funds undertaken by securities market markers.

The amendments, as set out in Appendix, will come into effect on Monday, 30 June 2025.

The marked-up version of the amendments to the Operational Procedures can be downloaded
from the "Rule Update - HKSCC Operational Procedures section of the HKEX website.
